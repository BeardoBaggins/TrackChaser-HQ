# 🏁 TrackChaser HQ
**Motorsport Attendance Tracker, Questbook, Bucket List & Round-Trip Budget Planner**

TrackChaser HQ is a Python desktop application built for race fans and track chasers to log attendance across NASCAR, ARCA Menards, and Grassroots short-track racing, conquer trackside quests, tour motorsport museums and race shops, and plan multi-stop race weekend budgets.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🏆 Multi-Series Schedule & Attendance Tracking
* **NASCAR National Series:** Full schedule tracking for the **Cup Series**, **O'Reilly Auto Parts Series**, and **FedEx Truck Series** with live schedule syncing from NASCAR.com (`2025`, `2026`, and `2027`).
* **ARCA Menards Series:** Dedicated divisions for **ARCA National**, **ARCA East**, and **ARCA West** with web schedule syncing.
* **Grassroots & Local Short Tracks:** Pre-loaded with 16 crown-jewel pavement and dirt events (*Snowball Derby, World 100, Knoxville Nationals, Chili Bowl, ValleyStar 300, Throwback Classic, All American 400, Winchester 400, Oxford 250, Slinger Nationals*, and more) plus a **Quick-Add Local Race Bar** to log any weekly short track, dirt oval, or regional race you attend.
* **Year-Locked Attendance History:** Stamps the exact season year (`2026`, `2027`, etc.) when you mark a race as attended and preserves your historical track count when syncing future schedules.

### 🔥 Same-Year Weekend Sweeps & Unique Track Counter
* **Weekend Sweeps ("Conquered"):** Automatically groups national series races taking place at the same track on the same weekend. Attending every race at that track during the same season year unlocks the **`[WEEKEND SWEEP CONQUERED!]`** badge.
* **Unique Physical Tracks Counter:** Tracks how many distinct speedways and dirt ovals you have visited across all series and years.

### 🛡️ RPG Quests, Track Food Challenges & "Side-Tracked" Traditions
* **Chaser Ranks & XP System:** Earn XP to level up from *Rookie Stripe Chaser* to *Hall of Fame Track Legend*.
* **Auto-Tracked Campaigns (14):** Live visual progress bars that automatically update from your attendance (*The NASCAR Grand Slam, Restrictor Plate Baron, Short Track Bully, Moonshine Heritage Trail, Red Clay Royalty, Race City USA Pilgrimage*, and more).
* **Track Food Challenges (34):** Checklist of iconic track concessions across the country—including the *$2 Martinsville Pink Hot Dog*, *Darlington Pimento Cheese*, *Eldora Pizza Burger & Giant Tenderloin*, and *Dawsonville Bully Burger*.
* **Side-Tracked Traditions (38+):** Meaningful trackside fan traditions (*Kissing the Bricks at Indy, signing the Charlotte/Daytona start/finish line, walking the 1948 Ponce Inlet North Turn beach sand, hiking the 1949 Occoneechee Ghost Track*, plus custom user goals).

### 🏛️ Attractions, Museums & Race Shops
* Pre-loaded directory of **36 iconic motorsport museums, halls of fame, and Charlotte/Mooresville race shops** with admission prices, tour details, and viewing-window notes.

### 🗺️ Round-Trip Route & Budget Planner
* **Multi-Stop Mileage & Return Leg:** Calculates sequential leg-by-leg driving mileage from your **Home Address → Stop #1 → Stop #2 → ... → Return Trip Home** and launches the full multi-stop route in Google Maps with one click.
* **Full Race Weekend Budget Tool:** Track per-stop **Base Admission/Tickets ($)**, **VIP/Garage Pass Upgrades ($)**, and **Parking ($)** alongside trip-wide expenses for **Hotel/Lodging**, **Fuel** (with 1-click Auto-Fuel estimation), **Food/Drinks**, **Souvenirs/Merch**, **Other**, and a **Target Budget** comparison.
* **Save & Export Itineraries:** Save multiple named trip plans to reload anytime, reorder stops (`▲` / `▼`), and export a formatted itinerary and budget breakdown to **`.txt`** or **`.csv`**.

### 🖼️ Custom Race & Track Logos
* Drop any `.png`, `.jpg`, or `.webp` image into the `custom_logos/` folder—or assign images directly in the app using **Set Race Logo...** and **Set Track Logo...**—to display stacked event and track logos in the table.
